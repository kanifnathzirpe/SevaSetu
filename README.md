# 🏥 SevaSetu

> AI-powered Rural Healthcare Platform for Smart India Hackathon 2026

## 📌 Problem Statement

**Title:**
Accessibility and Quality of Public Healthcare Services, Particularly in Rural and Underserved Areas

---

## Overview

SevaSetu AI is an integrated healthcare platform designed to improve healthcare accessibility, continuity of care, and quality of public health services in rural India.

The platform empowers patients, doctors, ASHA workers, hospitals, and government authorities through AI-assisted healthcare services, telemedicine, digital health records, and intelligent referral management.

---

## Core Features

- AI Symptom Triage
- Appointment & Queue Management
- Video Consultation
- Digital Health Records
- Medicine Availability
- Referral Tracking
- Doctor Dashboard
- ASHA Worker Dashboard
- Government Analytics
- Emergency Escalation
- Notifications & Follow-ups
